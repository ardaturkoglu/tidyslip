Tidyslip Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026 · Last updated: 12 October 2026 (version 0.5.1: in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland crash reports and usage statistics are off until you allow them) · Applies to the Tidyslip app for Android (com.tidyslip.app).

This policy explains what information the Tidyslip app handles, where it is stored and who can see it. In short: your jobs, customer details, photos and PDF reports stay on your device. The app has no accounts and no ads. When the app is online, it contacts our purchase service to check whether Pro is active. That check uses an anonymous ID, not your name or Google account, and never includes your jobs, customers or photos. If you leave the setting on, the app also sends crash reports and basic usage statistics to Google Firebase. These never include your jobs, customers, photos, business details or reports, and you can turn them off in Settings.

Tidyslip is developed and published by Arda Türkoğlu, an individual developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the app and for answering questions about this policy. You can reach us at turkogluarda0@gmail.com.

Data on your device

Everything you enter or add in Tidyslip is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Jobs, room sections, checklist items, notes and the optional customer name.
  • Photos you choose from your gallery. The app copies them into its own storage so they stay available even if you later remove the original from your gallery. The app only sees the photos you pick.
  • Photos you take in the app. Tidyslip uses your camera only while you are taking before and after photos for a job. Photos are saved only in Tidyslip's private storage on your device. Tidyslip does not upload them, does not add them to your photo gallery, and does not add your location to photos it takes. You can turn off camera access in Android Settings at any time and still add photos from your gallery.
  • Your business profile: business name, and if you add them, your logo, phone number, email address, website and review link. They appear on your reports as described in the app (some only with Pro).
  • Client sign-off (Pro): if you use it, the client's name (optional) and either their signature drawn on your device or, if they choose to sign with their name only, their name without a signature, with the date and time. It is saved with the job and printed on your Pro reports while the job has not changed since it was signed.
  • Your saved checklists (Pro). For each client, and overall, the app also remembers which app you last used to send a report so it can offer it again; this is stored only on your device, is not included in backups and is never sent anywhere. Your list of sent reports, including the name of the app each report was sent with (for example an email app), is stored with your jobs and is included in backups.
  • Before/after images you create from a photo pair. They are made on your device and can show your business name, logo and the photo caption. When you share one (Pro), the image file is kept in the app's temporary storage for at least 10 minutes so the receiving app can read it, and is deleted the next time you share an image or open Tidyslip. It is not added to your photo gallery.
  • PDF reports the app generates, and a local record of which reports you have exported (used for the free export allowance). Each PDF's file properties include the job title (if you left it blank, the client's name and job date) and the date the PDF was created.
  • Your Pro status as last confirmed by the purchase service, so Pro keeps working offline.

We do not have a server for this data and we cannot see it. The app does not include this data in Android's automatic cloud backup.

Your jobs, customer details, photos and reports leave your device only when you choose to send them:

  • Send, Share, Save PDF or Print: the report goes to the app, printer or location you pick (for example email, a messaging app, or a folder). From then on, that app or service's own privacy terms apply. The report contains what you put in the job (including the customer name and notes, if you added them), and its file properties include the job title and creation date.
  • "Created with Tidyslip" note (free version): PDFs made without Pro end with a small "Created with Tidyslip" note on the last page and a fixed link to Tidyslip's Google Play page. The link contains no job, customer or device information, only a fixed campaign label (utm_source=pdf, utm_medium=footer). If the person who receives the PDF opens the link, Google Play's privacy terms apply. If someone installs Tidyslip from this link, this label may reach Google Analytics as install information (see below). Pro removes the note.
  • Back up all jobs: creates a zip file at a location you choose. The zip is not encrypted or password-protected. It contains all your jobs, including customer names, notes, client sign-offs and the list of sent reports (with the names of the apps you sent them with), your business profile, logo and review link, your saved checklists, and your original photos. Original photos may contain metadata your camera stored, such as the date or location. Keep backup files somewhere safe that only you can access. Photos placed inside PDF reports are re-encoded without that metadata.
  • Share a before/after image (Pro): the image goes to the app you pick. From then on, that app's own privacy terms apply.
  • Review link and QR code (Pro): if you add a review link, your reports show it with a QR code. Whoever scans or opens it goes to the page you entered, under that site's terms.
  • Contact support: opens your email app with our address. The draft contains only the app and Android version; anything else is what you decide to write.

Data processed by service providers

Tidyslip offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Tidyslip Pro"). The first 3 report exports are free. Pro removes this limit and adds extra features described in the app, such as your logo and contact details on reports, client sign-off and saved checklists. Two service providers are involved:

  • Google Play (Google LLC) handles the payment, your payment details and the purchase receipt. We never see your card or payment details. Google's handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Google Play in-app review: after you have exported a couple of reports, the app may once ask Google Play to show its rating window. The rating and any review go directly to Google Play; the app does not see them, and the window never includes your jobs or photos.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. checks purchases with Google Play on our behalf and tells the app whether Pro is active.

    When: every time the app starts or comes back to the foreground while online, even if you have never bought anything, and when you open the Pro sheet, buy Pro or tap Restore purchases.

    What is sent:

    • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device the first time the app runs (it is not your name, email or Google account);
    • basic technical information needed for the request, such as the app version, Android version and device language;
    • only if you buy or restore: your purchase information for this app (product, purchase token, date, status, store country and currency).

    As with any internet request, RevenueCat's servers also see your device's IP address when the app connects.

    Why: to know whether Pro should be unlocked, to show the store price, to process purchases and restores, and to recover your purchase after a reinstall or on a new device.

    The RevenueCat software may also save the anonymous app user ID in Google's Block Store, a Google service that keeps small pieces of app data with your Google account and device backup, so the same ID can come back if you reinstall the app. RevenueCat's handling is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. RevenueCat is based in the United States, so this information may be processed there.

If you never buy Pro: RevenueCat holds only the anonymous app user ID and the technical request information above, with no purchase attached. We cannot link that ID to you, your name, your Google account or your jobs.

You can see your anonymous ID in the app under Settings → Anonymous purchase ID, and copy it if you want to contact us about it.

The product information the app receives from RevenueCat can also include a setting that chooses between a few built-in wordings of the Pro screen; no extra information is sent for this. We use this information only to provide, verify and restore purchases and to see overall purchase numbers in the RevenueCat dashboard. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising. Communication with these services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Crash reports and usage statistics (Google Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google LLC, to find crashes and to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used (for example how many reports are created or how often the Pro screen is shown).

What is sent: crash details (the error message and where in the app's code it happened, the state of the app's threads, device model, Android version, free memory and disk space, app version, whether you have Pro, and the last few in-app events before the crash); reports of a few unexpected errors the app handles itself, such as an unexpected error during a purchase, which contain only the type of error and where in the app's code it happened; a list of in-app events with limited fixed values (such as "report sent", "share" or "save", "free" or "pro"); automatic events that Firebase records for every app (such as first open, session start, screens viewed, time in the app, app updates and crashes, and, if you buy Pro, the purchase event with the product and price); general device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, device language, and an approximate location such as the country or city, derived from the IP address); information Google Play may provide about how the app was installed (for example the store campaign, if any); and a Firebase installation ID and an Analytics instance ID created for this app installation.

What is never sent: your jobs, customer names or addresses, notes, photos, report contents, business details, sign-offs or review links. Usage events contain only fixed values, and the app does not add your content or file names to crash reports.

Firebase is not used for advertising; the advertising ID is not collected and ad personalization is disabled.

Your choice: in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, and when the app can’t tell your region, crash reports and usage statistics stay off until you choose Allow on the first screen. Elsewhere this setting is on by default. You can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share usage and crash data. The app tells Google Analytics your choice through Google’s consent settings (analytics storage allowed or denied); advertising storage and ad personalization are always set to denied. When you turn it off, the app stops sending crash reports and usage statistics, deletes crash reports and usage data that were not sent yet, and deletes the Firebase installation ID (if your phone is offline, this is completed the next time the app starts).

Where and how long: Google may process this information in the United States. Crashlytics keeps crash reports and their identifiers for 90 days. Google Analytics keeps event-level and installation-level data for 2 months; aggregated statistics that do not identify an installation may be kept longer. Google's handling is described in the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number (unless you email us yourself).
  • No contents of your jobs, customer details, notes, photos, file names or PDF reports.
  • No precise location. The app does not request location permission. Google Analytics estimates an approximate location from the IP address, as described above.
  • No advertising ID and no ads. The app does not contain advertising software.
  • No contacts or microphone. The camera, and the motion sensor that tells the app how you hold the phone, are used only while you take photos in the app, and photos stay on your device unless you share them.

Retention and deletion

  • On your device: deleting a job removes it and its photos from the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes your jobs, photos, reports and settings from the device. The one exception is the anonymous app user ID: a copy may remain in Google Block Store with your Google account and device backup, and it can come back if you reinstall the app. Backup zips and PDFs you saved or shared elsewhere are not affected; delete them where you stored them.
  • RevenueCat: records are kept so that Pro can be verified and restored. To ask us to delete the RevenueCat record for your app, email turkogluarda0@gmail.com and include your anonymous ID (Settings → Anonymous purchase ID, copy and paste it) or, if you bought Pro, your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and appears in your Google Play receipt email). Without one of these we cannot find the record, because it is not linked to your name or email. We will delete the record in RevenueCat and reply to confirm. If you bought Pro, the purchase itself stays in your Google Play account, so you can still use Restore purchases later. If the app is still installed, or the ID comes back from Block Store after a reinstall, the app may use the same ID again the next time it checks Pro status.
  • Firebase: turn off Share usage and crash data to stop sending; this also resets the installation ID, so later data cannot be linked to earlier data. Data already sent is deleted after the periods above. Because this data is not linked to your name or email, we cannot look up which data is yours from an email request.
  • Google Play: Google keeps its own purchase records under its own policy. You can manage them in your Google account.
  • Emails to support: we keep support emails only as long as needed to answer you, and you can ask us to delete them.
