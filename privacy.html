Tidyslip Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026 · Last updated: 9 October 2026 (version 0.4.0: Take photo) · Applies to the Tidyslip app for Android (com.tidyslip.app).

This policy explains what information the Tidyslip app handles, where it is stored and who can see it. In short: your jobs, customer details, photos and PDF reports stay on your device. The app has no accounts and no ads. When the app is online, it contacts our purchase service to check whether Pro is active. That check uses an anonymous ID, not your name or Google account, and never includes your jobs, customers or photos.

Who we are

Tidyslip is developed and published by Arda Türkoğlu, an individual developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the app and for answering questions about this policy. You can reach us at turkoglua0@gmail.com.

Data on your device

Everything you enter or add in Tidyslip is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Jobs, room sections, checklist items, notes and the optional customer name.
  • Photos you choose from your gallery. The app copies them into its own storage so they stay available even if you later remove the original from your gallery. The app only sees the photos you pick.
  • Photos you take with Take photo. Take photo opens your device's camera app. The photo is saved only in Tidyslip's private storage on your device. Tidyslip does not add it to your photo gallery and does not upload it. Your camera app's own settings, such as location tagging, are controlled by that app. Tidyslip does not ask for camera permission.
  • PDF reports the app generates, and a local record of which reports you have exported (used for the free export allowance). Each PDF's file properties include the job title and the date the PDF was created.
  • Your Pro status as last confirmed by the purchase service, so Pro keeps working offline.

We do not have a server for this data and we cannot see it. The app does not include this data in Android's automatic cloud backup.

Your jobs, customer details, photos and reports leave your device only when you choose to send them:

  • Share or Save PDF: the report goes to the app or location you pick (for example email, a messaging app, or a folder). From then on, that app or service's own privacy terms apply. The report contains what you put in the job (including the customer name and notes, if you added them), and its file properties include the job title and creation date.
  • "Created with Tidyslip" note (free version): PDFs made without Pro end with a small "Created with Tidyslip" note on the last page and a fixed link to Tidyslip's Google Play page. The link contains no job, customer or device information, only a fixed campaign label (utm_source=pdf). If the person who receives the PDF opens the link, Google Play's privacy terms apply. Pro removes the note.
  • Back up all jobs: creates a zip file at a location you choose. The zip is not encrypted or password-protected. It contains all your jobs, including customer names and notes, and your original photos. Original photos may contain metadata your camera stored, such as the date or location. Keep backup files somewhere safe that only you can access. Photos placed inside PDF reports are re-encoded without that metadata.
  • Contact support: opens your email app with our address. The draft contains only the app and Android version; anything else is what you decide to write.

Data processed by service providers

Tidyslip offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Tidyslip Pro"). The first 3 report exports are free; other features do not require a purchase. Two service providers are involved:

  • Google Play (Google LLC) handles the payment, your payment details and the purchase receipt. We never see your card or payment details. Google's handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Google Play in-app review: after you have exported a couple of reports, the app may once ask Google Play to show its rating window. The rating and any review go directly to Google Play; the app does not see them, and the window never includes your jobs or photos.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. checks purchases with Google Play on our behalf and tells the app whether Pro is active.

    When: every time the app starts or comes back to the foreground while online, even if you have never bought anything, and when you open the Pro sheet, buy Pro or tap Restore purchases.

    What is sent:

    • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device the first time the app runs (it is not your name, email or Google account);
    • basic technical information needed for the request, such as the app version, Android version and device language;
    • only if you buy or restore: your purchase information for this app (product, purchase token, date, status, store country and currency).

    As with any internet request, RevenueCat's servers also see your device's IP address when the app connects.

    Why: to know whether Pro should be unlocked, to show the store price, to process purchases and restores, and to recover your purchase after a reinstall or on a new device.

    The RevenueCat software may also save the anonymous app user ID in Google's Block Store, a Google service that keeps small pieces of app data with your Google account and device backup, so the same ID can come back if you reinstall the app. RevenueCat's handling is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. RevenueCat is based in the United States, so this information may be processed there.

If you never buy Pro: RevenueCat holds only the anonymous app user ID and the technical request information above, with no purchase attached. We cannot link that ID to you, your name, your Google account or your jobs.

You can see your anonymous ID in the app under Settings → Anonymous purchase ID, and copy it if you want to contact us about it.

The product information the app receives from RevenueCat can also include a setting that chooses between a few built-in wordings of the Pro screen; no extra information is sent for this. We use this information only to provide, verify and restore purchases and to see overall purchase numbers in the RevenueCat dashboard. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising. Communication with these services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

What we don't collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number (unless you email us yourself).
  • No contents of your jobs, customer details, notes, photos, file names or PDF reports.
  • No location. The app does not request location permission.
  • No advertising ID and no ads. The app does not contain advertising software.
  • No contacts, camera, microphone or other device sensors.
  • No crash reports or usage analytics in the current version.

Retention and deletion

  • On your device: deleting a job removes it and its photos from the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes your jobs, photos, reports and settings from the device. The one exception is the anonymous app user ID: a copy may remain in Google Block Store with your Google account and device backup, and it can come back if you reinstall the app. Backup zips and PDFs you saved or shared elsewhere are not affected; delete them where you stored them.
  • RevenueCat: records are kept so that Pro can be verified and restored. To ask us to delete the RevenueCat record for your app, email turkoglua0@gmail.com and include your anonymous ID (Settings → Anonymous purchase ID, copy and paste it) or, if you bought Pro, your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and appears in your Google Play receipt email). Without one of these we cannot find the record, because it is not linked to your name or email. We will delete the record in RevenueCat and reply to confirm. If you bought Pro, the purchase itself stays in your Google Play account, so you can still use Restore purchases later. If the app is still installed, or the ID comes back from Block Store after a reinstall, the app may use the same ID again the next time it checks Pro status.
  • Google Play: Google keeps its own purchase records under its own policy. You can manage them in your Google account.
  • Emails to support: we keep support emails only as long as needed to answer you, and you can ask us to delete them.

Children

Tidyslip is a work tool for adults who run cleaning and similar service businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, email us and we will delete that correspondence.

Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
