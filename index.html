Three steps after every cleaning job

  1. Take the before photos

    Start a job with a ready-made checklist (Standard, Deep or Move-out), then use the in-app camera or pick photos from your gallery.

  2. Clean room by room

    Tick off each task as you go: kitchen, bathroom, office, or whatever you name the room.

  3. Take the after photos and send

    A see-through copy of the before photo helps you match the same angle. Then check what’s missing, add a message for your client and send the PDF.

Questions about the cleaning report app

Can I make a cleaning report PDF without an account?

Yes. There is no sign-up. Pro is tied to your Google Play account, and you can restore it with “Restore purchases”.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, for creating jobs, previewing and exporting reports. Buying or restoring Pro needs a connection.

Can I use before and after photos I already took?

Yes. Take photos with the in-app camera or add them from your phone’s gallery; each pair sits side by side.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Tidyslip is available for Android. Your clients can open the PDF reports on any phone or computer.

Are the dates and photos in the report verified?

No. A Tidyslip report is a simple record you create and share. Dates, photos and sign-offs come from you and your phone. They are not independently verified or certified.

Is the client sign-off legally binding?

No. It shows your client saw the report. It isn’t a legal contract and isn’t verified.

Do my clients need the app?

No. They get a PDF that opens on any phone or computer.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Google Play account.

Can I add my review link?

Yes, with Pro. Your link and a QR code appear at the end of each report, with a neutral “How did we do?” line.

Which languages does Tidyslip support?

The app and its PDF reports are available in English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (Latin America and Spain), German and French.

Does Tidyslip collect my data?

Only usage and crash data, which you can turn off in Settings, and an anonymous ID to check Pro. Your jobs, clients and photos are never sent. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my cleaning jobs to a new phone?

Use “Back up all jobs” in Settings to save a zip file, then “Restore from backup” on the new phone.
